## v0.8.2 — Inline Result Details

**Date:** 2026-06-10

### Changes

- Removed the hidden results Details accordion.
- Replaced the accordion with always-visible inline result rows.
- Kept Gross Pay and Take-Home Pay as the major summary rows.
- Kept Regular Pay, Premium Pay, Shift Differential Pay, Benefit / Leave Pay, Other Earnings, Specialty Pay, Total Paid Hours, Taxes, Deductions, Other Adjustments, and Target Pay details visible without requiring a click.
- Updated build labels, footer version, cache-busting references, and documentation to v0.8.2.
- Started Paycheck public/admin changelog split.
- Footer changelog link now points to `PUBLIC_CHANGELOG.md`.
- No shared asset content changes.
