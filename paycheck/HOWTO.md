# Paycheck Calculator How-To

## Basic Use

1. Enter regular hours.
2. Enter hourly rate.
3. Choose currency and pay period.
4. Add premium hours only if they apply.
5. Add paid leave only if it appears on the paycheck.
6. Add taxes, deductions, or other adjustments as needed.
7. Use Calculate Paycheck.

## Result Details

Starting in v0.8.2, the results breakdown is visible directly on the page. There is no hidden Details accordion.

Visible rows include:

- Regular Pay
- Premium Pay
- Shift Differential Pay
- Benefit / Leave Pay
- Other Earnings
- Specialty Pay
- Total Paid Hours
- Estimated Taxes
- Deductions
- Other Adjustments
- Target Pay details
