Employee Read Foundation

Read-only employee records now flow through a service, repository, and JSON adapter boundary. This prepares the app for a future Coolify API + Postgres adapter without tying the UI to a database.

Assignments Foundation

Assignments connect employees to operational work such as consoles, posts, beats, stations, units, or desks before minimum staffing and schedule generation are built.

Minimum Staffing Foundation

Minimum staffing rules define how many people are required for each assignment, role, shift, location, and day before leave approval, overtime posting, mandation, and schedule generation are built.

Agency Profile Foundation

v1.8.0 keeps the Employee Service and repository boundary intact while defining the future Coolify API route shape for /health and /employees.

Agency Profile Foundation

Agency-defined settings and vocabulary become the source for employee profiles, shift patterns, coverage rules, benefits, exceptions, and future rule-engine behavior.

Employee Profile Foundation

Employee profiles use agency-defined departments, positions, shift groups, qualifications, exception types, and benefit types instead of hard-coded assumptions.

Pattern Foundation

Patterns describe repeating work/off cycles, normal shifts, short days, short weeks, paid minutes, and break rules before final schedule generation exists.

Pattern Cycle Preview

Short days are modeled by cycle position, not hard-coded weekdays. The display order also follows the agency profile week-start setting.

Event Foundation

Events describe what changes the expected pattern: time off, sick calls, overtime, mandation, training, trades, callbacks, holidays, and administrative overrides.

Event Behavior Preview

Event types should be grouped by behavior: removes from coverage, adds to coverage, changes role, changes pay, uses benefit time, requires approval, or requires an audit trail.

Benefit Ledger Foundation

Benefit balances should be calculated from ledger entries. Accruals, usage, corrections, payouts, carryovers, and manual adjustments should never silently overwrite a balance.

Benefit Rule Preview

Agency rules should support monthly accruals, per-paycheck accruals, annual banks, seniority tiers, manual adjustments, and projected balances.

Rule Engine Foundation

Rules evaluate agency policy against employees, patterns, events, benefits, coverage needs, exceptions, and audit history. The goal is not just to decide what happens, but to explain why.

Rule Evaluation Preview

Future rules should evaluate priority, exceptions, outcome, and explanation before changing a schedule, mandation list, benefit balance, coverage warning, or bid award.

Agency Template Preview

Industries should start from editable templates, not hard-coded modes. Dispatch, police, fire, EMS, nursing, retail, manufacturing, and office teams should all be rule-configurable.

Coverage Requirement Model

Coverage should support minimum, target, and maximum staffing by role, qualification, location, unit, day, and time block.

Coverage Engine Foundation

Coverage compares scheduled staffing against minimum, target, and maximum requirements by role, qualification, location, day, and time block.

Open Spot Preview

Numbered spots show how many seats are filled, open, below minimum, near target, or above maximum before a final scheduler exists.

Schedule Views Foundation

The same engine should power different views: day, week, month, personal, coverage, and system inspector. These are previews, not final layouts.

System Inspector Note

The current wall of cards is intentionally engine-facing. Later releases should turn these debug panels into admin screens, employee views, and supervisor dashboards.

Fairness Engine Foundation

Fairness compares history, rules, and exceptions so overtime, mandates, weekends, holidays, callbacks, and seniority outcomes can be explained instead of guessed.

Fairness Snapshot Preview

Sample employees show how future reports could compare OT minutes, mandate counts, weekend load, active exceptions, and seniority adjustments.

Seniority Ledger Preview

Hire date, seniority date, and effective seniority can differ. Future rules should calculate effective seniority from auditable adjustments, not silent edits.

Explainability Foundation

Explanations should turn facts, rules, events, history, and audience context into clear reasons for coverage, mandation, benefits, seniority, eligibility, and fairness outcomes.

Explanation Level Preview

The same outcome may need employee-safe, supervisor, and admin/audit explanations with different levels of detail.

Mandation Foundation

Mandation is forced overtime. It needs its own rotation list, counts, eligibility checks, skip reasons, and explanations instead of being treated as normal overtime or benefit time.

Mandate Rotation Preview

Sample rotation entries show who is next, who is skipped, and why. Future agency rules decide whether skipped employees keep their spot, move down, or leave the pool.

Operational Trait Planning

Employee traits, including gender, may be needed for police, fire, corrections, transport, search, housing, or safety rules, but should only be used when tied to documented operational needs.

Bidding and Opportunity Foundation

Bidding should support shift bids, vacation bids, overtime opportunity bids, rounds, eligibility, seniority, fairness, tie breakers, awards, and audit trails.

Voluntary OT Request Preview

Voluntary OT requests are employee-initiated requests to work extra time, submitted for later management review.

Posted OT Opportunity Preview

Posted OT opportunities are management-created openings that eligible employees can volunteer or bid for before awards are reviewed and published.

Bid Award Preview

Awards should preserve eligibility, seniority, fairness, coverage, tie-breaker, and audit explanations.
