## Version 0.6.2

**Date:** 2026-06-06  
**Time:** 09:00 UTC

### Theme

Smart Hours

### Changes

- Replaced "Overtime After" input with Pay Period.
- Added Weekly pay period support.

- Added Semi-Monthly pay period support.
- Added Monthly pay period support.
