=====================================================
Signal Labs Overtime Calculator
ROADMAP
=====================================================

Last Updated:
2026-06-06
03:30 UTC

Current Version:
v0.5

Purpose:

The Signal Labs Overtime Calculator is intended to provide
a clean, modern, and useful way to estimate overtime pay,
gross pay, and eventually take-home pay, income goals,
and advanced overtime scenarios.

This roadmap represents current plans and priorities.

Plans are subject to change.

Features may be moved, removed, deferred, expanded, or
reworked between versions as development progresses.

Additional updates, improvements, and entirely new features
are always possible.
